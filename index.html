👨‍🏫 Biography

Yixiu Liu is an Associate Professor and master's supervisor at the School of Cyber Science and Engineering, Hangzhou Dianzi University. He received his Ph.D. in Control Science and Engineering from Northeastern University, with a focus on Pattern Recognition and Intelligent Systems. He also spent two years as a CSC visiting scholar at the University of California, Riverside. His research interests include computer vision, artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, cross-modal person re-identification, multimodal intention understanding, video anomaly detection and localization, 3D human editing, camouflaged object detection, and AI model backdoor attack and defense.

🔍 Research Interests

Computer Vision: Cross-modal person re-identification, cloth-changing person re-identification, text-based person re-identification, camouflaged object detection, and salient object detection. Multimodal Artificial Intelligence: Multimodal intention recognition, video anomaly detection and localization, and robust visual understanding in complex scenarios. 3D Human Editing: 3D human modeling, pose and shape editing, clothing replacement, motion transfer, and realistic rendering based on NeRF, 3D Gaussian Splatting, and large models. AI Security: Backdoor attack and defense, hidden trigger analysis, and trustworthy AI in security-critical applications.
